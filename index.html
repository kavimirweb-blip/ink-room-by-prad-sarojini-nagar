Questions

Before you book.

How long does a set take?

Gel polish takes about 45 minutes, art sets 90 minutes and extensions around 2 hours. The time is shown on each service.

Do I need to pay in advance?

No. Booking on WhatsApp is free and you pay at the studio. (The studio can add a small deposit for long sets if it wants.)

Can I bring a reference photo?

Yes. Send it on the same WhatsApp chat after you book, and the artist will confirm what is possible.

What if I need to reschedule?

Just message on the same chat. Please let the studio know a few hours before your slot.
